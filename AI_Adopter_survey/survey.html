Purpose

This survey contains 21 candidate items for an Artificial Intelligence (AI) adopter “type”. The items were informed from a qualitative study (Callari and Puppione 2025). At the end of the survey you will be provided a personalized report from the 21–item scale, however, the final validated measure will most likely consist of fewer items (your responses will help us refine the 21–items).
